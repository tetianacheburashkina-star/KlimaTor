Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to renovate my home for installation? ▼

No. Modern Zigbee and Wi-Fi smart radiator thermostats, relays, and sensors install cleanly without tearing down walls.

Does KlimaTor work if the internet goes down? ▼

Yes. In Local Gateway mode, KlimaTor communicates directly with your local hub over your home network.

Can installers or developers license the KlimaTor UI? ▼

Yes, KlimaTor supports custom branding and connects directly to Home Assistant REST API or MQTT/WebSocket bridges.
